One onboarding model, every market

Factorial is adding customers, countries and products faster than most post-sale teams can absorb. This kit is how I would run onboarding as one shared model, with a short module for what changes in each market.

One core pathFive stages, sized by customer segment, the same in every country.
A real handoffNothing goes to onboarding until the scope is clear and sold honestly.
Local where it mattersMarket modules for payroll, compliance, language and common blockers.

The core path

    Sales-to-onboarding handoff check

    Reviews of Factorial repeatedly mention features that were promised in sales but missing at implementation. The fix is a short check before the deal is signed, filled in together by the account executive and an onboarding lead. Tick what is true for the deal.

      Market modules

      Each market runs the same five stages. These pages list what I would check locally before kickoff. They are a checklist of questions, not claims about what Factorial supports today.

      Time-to-value tracker

      Add each onboarding to see days to go-live against the target for its segment, and compare markets. Data stays in your browser. Export and import as CSV.

      How the team runs it

      Monday: portfolio review. Every onboarding past its stage target gets an owner and a next step. Ten minutes per market.
      Wednesday: blocker desk. Onboarding, product and payroll specialists clear cross-market blockers in one place, so Germany and Brazil don't solve the same problem twice.
      Monthly: market module update. Each market lead adds what slowed customers down, so the module gets sharper with every go-live.
      Quarterly: segment reset. Stage targets move up or down based on real median days, not hopes.
